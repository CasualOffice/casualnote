# Governance

Adapted from OpenPresent's `GOVERNANCE.md`. The project sits under the CasualOffice organisation, alongside OpenCalc and OpenPresent.

## Authority

1. **The owner's instructions,** recorded in [docs/03](docs/03-OWNER-INSTRUCTIONS.md).
2. **Accepted ADRs** in [docs/08](docs/08-ADR-REGISTER.md).
3. **The design documents** indexed in [docs/00](docs/00-README.md).

Only the owner closes a phase ([docs/05](docs/05-PHASES-AND-TASKS.md)).

## Roles

- **Owner:** sets direction, closes phases, holds decisions that need a lawyer (`DEC-08`).
- **Maintainers:** none yet.
- **Contributors:** welcome once the repository is public, under [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Commitments

- Everything here stays Apache-2.0: engine, server, apps, SDK.
- No enterprise edition, licence key, watermark or commercial folder in this repository.
- The export format stays open to implement by anyone, and a reader for every shipped major version is kept permanently.
